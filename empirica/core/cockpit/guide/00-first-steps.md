# 0. First steps: finding your way around

Read this page first. It covers the few things you do with your hands; everything else in
the guide builds on them.

## The cockpit window

The cockpit is one window full of text, often with a dark background. It is called a
**terminal**. It is split into:

- **tabs** along the top, one per practice;
- **boxes** inside each tab, one per AI practitioner (usually one or two side by side);
- under each box, a **line of commands**: click it to open the cockpit's menu (page 10).

## Two kinds of typing

1. **Talking to a practitioner.** Click inside its box, type in plain words, press
   **Enter**. That is a message to the AI, like a chat.
2. **Running a command** such as `empirica cockpit launch`. You only do this *outside* the
   cockpit, in an ordinary terminal window: open the **Terminal** app (on a Mac: press
   Cmd+Space, type "Terminal", press Enter), type the command exactly as written, press
   **Enter**. If you type a command into a practitioner's box instead, the AI receives it as
   a message; it will usually understand and run it for you, which is fine too.

The box with a highlighted border is the one you are typing into. Click another box to
switch.

## Key sequences

The cockpit's own shortcuts start with **Ctrl+b**. They are pressed one after another, not
all together. For `Ctrl+b e R`:

1. Hold **Ctrl**, press **b**, let go of both.
2. Press **e**.
3. Press **R** (capital: hold **Shift** and press **r**).

`?` also needs **Shift**. Tabs are numbered from 1 as shown on the tab; `Ctrl+b` then **2**
goes to tab 2. You can always click a tab, or click the line under a box and choose from
the menu, instead.

## Answering the practitioner's questions

Sometimes a practitioner shows a small menu, for example *1. Yes  2. No*, or a list with
one option marked **(Recommended)**. Use the **up/down arrow keys** to pick, then press
**Enter**, or type the number. You can always type your own answer instead.

## Stopping, scrolling, copying

- **Stop** a practitioner in the middle of something: press **Esc**. It stops and waits.
- **Scroll back** through the conversation: use the mouse wheel. If the box seems stuck
  after scrolling, press **q** to return to the live view.
- **Copy and paste**: select text with the mouse to copy; paste with right-click or the usual
  paste shortcut.

## Where its work ends up

Documents and files a practitioner writes are saved in the practice's folder on this
computer. Ask it "where did you save that?" and it gives you the exact place, or a link
you can open.
