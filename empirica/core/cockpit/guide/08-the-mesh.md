# 8. Practices talking to each other

## The mesh

Your practices can send each other messages. Together they form the **mesh**. The outreach
practice can ask the website practice a question; a support practice can tell another that a
fix is ready. This lets each practice stay focused on its own area and still work as a team.

## Two kinds of message

| Kind | What it is | Needs your approval? |
|---|---|---|
| **Collaboration** | a question, a heads-up, sharing a finding, a discussion | no, it arrives directly |
| **Request** | a request to *do* something: change something, publish, take a decision | yes, a person approves or declines it |

A collaboration can never make another practice act. Only a request can, and only after a
person has approved it.

## Approving a request

Requests wait for your approval in the Empirica extension, the Empirica panel in your
web browser. Each one shows who is asking, what they want done, and why. Approve it, decline it,
or ask for a change. If you decline, say why in a sentence; the practice that asked learns
from it.

A practitioner treats a request from another practice as information, not an order. Even
when another practice asks politely, anything that changes things, publishes or touches a
client waits for your approval.

## What you will see

- Messages arrive as short notes in the practitioner's conversation; it reads them and
  either answers, does the approved work, or tells you it needs your decision.
- When it finishes work another practice asked for, it replies to say so, with proof. That
  closes the request on the other side.
- The **I inbox** command under each box turns orange when messages are waiting for an answer.
