# 11. Joining, and adding a practice

## Your first day

Most of the setup is done for you by the support team. Your part is short.

**Done for you**
1. An account on the Empirica server.
2. Your computer connected to the team's private network, so support can help you remotely.
3. Empirica and the AI apps (Claude Code, and ecodex if you use ChatGPT) installed.
4. Your practices created, each with its folder and notebook.
5. Your cockpit set up with those practices in it.

**What you do**
1. **Sign in once.** Open a terminal (page 0), type `empirica auth login`, press Enter. A page
   opens in your web browser; sign in with your Empirica account [check: which account and
   how the page looks] and confirm. After that the sign-in renews itself; the **L** button
   turns orange if it ever needs doing again.
2. **Open your cockpit.** In the terminal, type `empirica cockpit launch`, press Enter.
3. **Read** pages 0, 1, 2 and 7 of this guide.
4. **Start small.** Pick one practice and give it a small, real task. Ask the practitioner to
   explain what it is doing as it goes.

## Adding a practice

A new practice makes sense when a new area of work deserves its own notebook: a new client,
a new product, a separate stream of writing. Keep related work together in one practice;
split when the work, the people involved or the confidentiality differ.

To get one, ask the support team for "a new practice for X". They create it, connect it so
other practices can send it messages, and add it to your cockpit.

## Getting help

- In the cockpit: click the line under any box and choose **? help and guide** (or press
  `Ctrl+b e ?`) to open this guide.
- Ask any practitioner: "how do I ...?" It can read this guide too.
- For anything about your computer, signing in or the cockpit itself: ask the support team
  [check: how a new person reaches support: a named contact, or a support tab in the cockpit].

Administrators: cockpit settings and practice setup are in page 12.
