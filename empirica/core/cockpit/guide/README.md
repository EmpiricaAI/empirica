# Working with Empirica: a guide for everyone

This guide is for anyone who works in an Empirica cockpit, whether or not you write code.
Each page covers one topic and takes a few minutes to read. On your first day read pages
0, 1, 2 and 7; read the rest when they come up, and come back to any page when you need it.

In the cockpit, click the line under any box and choose **? help and guide**, or press
`Ctrl+b e ?`, to open this guide. Scroll with the mouse wheel, the arrow keys or the arrow
buttons at the top; click **Close** at the top, or press `q`, to close it. Click a page
number in the list below to jump to that page.

## Three words you will meet everywhere

- **Practice**: one area of work with its own memory, for example "outreach" or "website".
  It lives in a folder and keeps everything it learns.
- **Practitioner**: the AI working in a practice right now. It can be Claude or ChatGPT;
  both can work in the same practice and share its memory.
- **Cockpit**: the window that holds all your practices, with their tabs along the top
  and the cockpit's commands on the line under each box.
